# Lessons — LIVE

**The lab's append-only lessons ledger.** Each apparatus or process lesson is recorded once, in plain language, dated to when it was learned, with a pointer to the logbook entry or mission log where the full incident is told. Corrections are dated additions — the original entry is never edited.

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — [lesson] <plain title: what was learned, not what broke>
<What happened, in plain words, and the fix. Pointer: `ops/missions/<slug>.md` or `record/logbook/lab.md`, the dated entry that tells the full incident.>
```

## Corrections

None yet. A correction to any lesson above lands here as a new, dated entry — the original entry is never edited.
