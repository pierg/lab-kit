# Findings — LIVE

**The single source every number re-derives from.** No number enters a plan, a page, a paper or a post except by citing a row here, and every row carries its origin anchor and the command that reproduces it. Corrections land **here first**, dated, then propagate outward.

**A row that turns out to be wrong is struck through, not deleted** — it may already have been cited. Everything else that is superseded is deleted and recorded in `RETIRED.md` with a recovery SHA; git is the archive.

**Tiers are never blurred.** A reviewer-gated result and a lab finding under a locked pre-registration are both real and are not equal in weight. Prose must not pool them.

**The row is the interface.** It carries what a citer needs and nothing else; the defense — predictions as scored, the reviewer's verdict, the anomalies, the disclosures, the long-form bound — lives at the row's `Defense:` path and is never summarized back into the row.
